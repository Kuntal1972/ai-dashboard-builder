📖 Prompt Writing Guide

Write clear, specific prompts to get the best dashboards. The more detail you give, the better the result.

🧭 General Instructions
  • Start with a high-level description: "Create a sales dashboard…"
  • Name the exact columns from your dataset (column names are shown in the left panel)
  • Specify aggregations: sum, average, count, max, min
  • List all the charts and KPIs you want — AI builds exactly what you describe
  • Use commas to separate multiple chart requests in a single prompt
Example
Create a sales performance dashboard with KPI cards for total revenue and order count, a bar chart for revenue by region, and a line chart for monthly sales trend.
📌 KPI Cards

KPI cards show a single aggregated number. Specify the column and the aggregation.

Pattern KPI card for [aggregation] of [column name]
  • KPI card for total Revenue
  • KPI card for average Order Value
  • KPI card for count of Orders
  • KPI card for max Profit Margin
💡 Tip: Group multiple KPIs together — "KPI cards for total Revenue, count of Orders, and average Discount"
📊 Chart Requests

Name the chart type, the axis columns, and the aggregation.

Pattern [chart type] of [aggregation of Y-column] by [X-column]
Barbar chart for total Sales by Region
Lineline chart for monthly Revenue trend by Month
Areaarea chart for cumulative Revenue over Time
Piepie chart for Revenue share by Category
Donutdonut chart for order mix by Product Type
Scatterscatter plot of Revenue vs Profit by Customer
H-Barhorizontal bar chart for top 10 Products by Sales
Tabledata table showing Region, Sales, and Orders
🔍 Filter & Slicer Requests

Filters appear in the left panel and let you drill down interactively.

Pattern add a filter/slicer for [column name]
  • add a filter for Region
  • add slicers for Category and Year
  • include date range filter for Order Date
💡 Tip: Filters work best on categorical columns (text) and date columns. Avoid filtering on high-cardinality numeric columns.
✨ Full Example Prompt
Copy & adapt this
Create a sales dashboard with: – KPI cards for total Revenue, count of Orders, average Order Value, and max Discount – Bar chart for total Revenue by Region – Area chart for monthly Revenue trend by Month – Donut chart for Revenue share by Product Category – Horizontal bar chart for top 10 Customers by Revenue – Data table showing Region, Total Sales, Orders, and Avg Value – Filters for Region, Category, and Year
🔄 Using Refine

After building, use the Refine button to make targeted changes without rebuilding everything:

  • "Change the bar chart to a horizontal bar chart"
  • "Add a KPI card for total Profit"
  • "Remove the scatter plot and add a treemap for sales by sub-category"
  • "Make the trend chart an area chart"
